# Vinyl Crate

Vinyl Crate tracks each collector's personal collection of vinyl records.

## Language

**Collector**:
A person with an account and a private crate of records.
_Avoid_: User when describing the music collection.

**Crate**:
The collection of records owned by one collector.
_Avoid_: Inventory when describing the collector's collection.

**Record**:
An individual vinyl item cataloged in a crate, including its condition and price.
_Avoid_: Album when referring to the collector-owned item.
